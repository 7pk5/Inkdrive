# Chrome Web Store listing text (copy and paste)

## Name
Inkdrive

## Summary (max 132 characters)
Save your excalidraw.com drawings to your own private cloud, with autosave, folders and tags.

## Category / language
Productivity / English

## Detailed description
Inkdrive adds private cloud storage to excalidraw.com. Sign in once with Google and your drawings follow you to any computer, instead of living only in one browser.

HOW IT WORKS
• Open excalidraw.com and click the Inkdrive button next to the library.
• Continue with Google. There is no new password to remember.
• Draw as usual. Autosave keeps your drawing up to date in your account.
• Open the side panel to see all your drawings and pick one up on any computer.

WHAT YOU GET
• Autosave, on by default, and the latest change is saved before you switch files
• New drawing, and upload of existing .excalidraw files
• Folders, tags, search, starred and recent
• Version history: go back to an earlier version of a drawing
• Make a copy, download as .excalidraw, trash and restore
• A clean, calm panel that follows your light or dark theme

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Every account sees only its own drawings. Nothing is shared with other people.
• Drawings are encrypted in storage, and all traffic uses HTTPS.
• Sign-in is only through Google. We receive your name, email and picture, nothing else from your Google account.
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Honest note: this is not end-to-end encryption. The server has to read a stored drawing to show it to you, so the person running the service could technically read stored drawings.

PERMISSIONS, IN PLAIN WORDS
• excalidraw.com: reads the drawing on the canvas to save it, and puts a saved drawing back on the canvas. The extension does not run on any other site.
• Sign-in and storage: keeps you signed in and remembers your settings.
• Side panel: shows your file list beside the canvas.

Inkdrive is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Excalidraw.

Privacy policy: https://inkdrive.duckdns.org/privacy
